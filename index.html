ABOUT VERTICAL FARMING

Farming Doesn't Always Need a Field.

Vertical farming is a modern way of growing crops in vertically stacked layers. It allows farmers to produce food in smaller spaces using controlled environments.

GROWING METHODS

Three Ways to Grow

Different systems with one common goal: growing fresh food efficiently.

01 / HYDROPONICS

Growing in Water

Plants grow with their roots receiving nutrient-rich water instead of soil.

02 / AEROPONICS

Growing in Air

Plant roots hang in the air and receive nutrients through a fine mist.

03 / AQUAPONICS

Fish and Plants

Fish and plants work together in a system that recycles nutrients.

WHY VERTICAL FARMING?

Vertical farming can help grow fresh food in places where farmland is limited. It also allows growers to monitor light, water and temperature. However, energy and equipment costs still matter.

INSIDE A VERTICAL FARM

How Does It Actually Work?

A vertical farm uses technology and controlled conditions to help plants grow throughout the year.

🌱

1. Seed Planting

Seeds are placed in small growing trays with suitable growing material to begin their growth.

💧

2. Nutrient Supply

Plants receive water mixed with essential nutrients that support healthy growth.

💡

3. Light and Temperature

LED lights and climate-control systems help maintain suitable growing conditions.

🥬

4. Harvesting

When crops become mature, they are harvested, cleaned and prepared for sale.

From Seed to Harvest

Every stage is carefully monitored.

24/7

Environmental monitoring can run continuously.

365

Potential for year-round crop production.

Less Soil

Many systems grow plants without traditional field soil.
